Add `duplicate()` and `nudge()` plus keyboard shortcuts for duplicate (`Ctrl/Cmd+D`), arrow-key nudge (1px; Shift moves 10px), and zoom in/out/reset (`Ctrl/Cmd +`, `Ctrl/Cmd -`, `Ctrl/Cmd 0`). Duplicates get fresh IDs, remap group children, bound text and arrow bindings that stay inside the duplicated set, stack above the content and become the selection. Nudge moves groups, paths, lines, arrows and bound text consistently, detaching arrow bindings whose targets stay behind.
