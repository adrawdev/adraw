Add a tldraw-style sticky Notes tool (`N`). Clicking places a 200×200 note and opens its inline editor; dragging sizes it. Notes render with a folded corner, a soft drop shadow, centered wrapping text, a configurable background (`fillColor`), ink (`strokeColor`) and text styling, and grow taller when their text no longer fits. Exposes `NoteElement`, `createNote` and `createNoteTool`.
